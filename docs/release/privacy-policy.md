# KidsAI 隱私權政策

生效日期：＜上架日期＞
適用版本：KidsAI 首版（0.1）

KidsAI 是給 6–8 歲孩子玩的 App（5 歲的孩子可以在大人陪伴下一起玩），讓孩子認識「AI 會猜、會錯、可以被教」。我們把這個 App 設計成**不收集任何資料**。

## 我們收集什麼

**什麼都不收集。** KidsAI 不收集、不儲存、不分享任何個人資料或使用資料，包括孩子或家長的姓名、聲音、照片、位置、聯絡資訊、裝置識別碼，也不收集孩子在 App 裡選了什麼、玩了多久。

## App 怎麼運作

- **不連網。** 所有關卡、圖片和文字都內建在 App 裡，App 不會連到網路，也不會把任何東西傳出裝置。
- **進度不保存。** 孩子玩到哪一關、解鎖了哪些島，只在 App 開著的時候記在記憶體裡；關掉 App 就清除，不會寫進裝置。
- **語音在裝置上產生。** 旁白由 iPhone 內建的語音合成念出來，在裝置上完成，不需要網路。
- **不使用麥克風、相機、照片、定位或通訊錄**，所以 App 不會要求這些權限。

## 沒有第三方

KidsAI 沒有廣告，沒有分析或追蹤工具，沒有崩潰回報工具，也沒有任何第三方套件。App 裡沒有通往外部網站或其他 App 的連結，也沒有 App 內購買。

## 兒童隱私

KidsAI 是為兒童設計的 App。因為我們不收集任何資料，所以沒有孩子的資料可以查閱、更正、匯出或刪除。刪除 App 就會移除 App 本身；App 本來就沒有在裝置上留下孩子的資料。

Apple 處理 App Store 下載等資訊的方式，依 Apple 自己的隱私權政策，不在本政策範圍內。

## 政策變更

如果之後的版本加入會用到資料的功能，我們會在推出前先更新這份政策，並在 App 的更新說明裡告訴家長改了什麼。

# KidsAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: <release date>
Applies to: KidsAI first release (0.1)

KidsAI is an app for children aged 6–8 (5-year-olds can play together with an adult) that helps them understand that "AI guesses, can be wrong, and can be taught." We designed the app to **collect no data at all**.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** KidsAI does not collect, store, or share any personal data or usage data. This includes names, voices, photos, location, contact information, and device identifiers of children or parents. We also do not collect what children choose in the app or how long they play.

## How the app works

- **No internet.** All levels, pictures, and text are built into the app. The app never connects to the internet and never sends anything off the device.
- **Progress is not saved.** Which level a child reached and which islands are unlocked are kept in memory only while the app is open. They are cleared when the app is closed and are never written to the device.
- **Speech is generated on the device.** Narration uses the iPhone's built-in speech synthesis, entirely on the device, without the internet.
- **No microphone, camera, photos, location, or contacts.** The app does not ask for these permissions.

## No third parties

KidsAI has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no crash-reporting tools, and no third-party code libraries. The app has no links to outside websites or other apps, and no in-app purchases.

## Children's privacy

KidsAI is made for children. Because we collect no data, there is no child data to review, correct, export, or delete. Deleting the app removes the app itself; the app never leaves a child's data on the device.

How Apple handles information such as App Store downloads is covered by Apple's own privacy policy, not this one.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that uses data, we will update this policy before that version is released and tell parents what changed in the app's release notes.
