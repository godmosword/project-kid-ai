# KidsAI 隱私權政策

生效日期：＜上架日期＞
適用範圍：KidsAI 0.1 起、直到本政策下次更新前的所有版本

KidsAI 由＜開發者名稱＞開發與提供（以下稱「我們」），是給 6–8 歲孩子玩的 App（5 歲的孩子可以在大人陪伴下一起玩），讓孩子認識「AI 會猜、會錯、可以被教」。我們把這個 App 設計成不收集任何資料。

## 我們收集什麼

**App 本身什麼都不收集。** KidsAI 不收集、不儲存、不分享任何個人資料或使用資料，包括孩子或家長的姓名、聲音、照片、位置、聯絡資訊、裝置識別碼，也不收集孩子在 App 裡選了什麼、玩了多久。

## App 怎麼運作

- **不連網。** 所有關卡、圖片和文字都內建在 App 裡，App 不會連到網路，也不會把任何東西傳出裝置。
- **進度不保存。** 孩子玩到哪一關、解鎖了哪些島，只暫時記在 App 執行中的記憶體裡，App 不會把它存進裝置。從多工畫面把 App 滑掉、裝置重新開機，或系統在背景把 App 關掉時，進度就會清除，下次從頭開始；只是切到別的 App 再回來，通常還能接著玩。
- **旁白是裝置內建的語音。** 旁白由 Apple 裝置內建的語音合成在裝置上念出來（不是真人錄音），不需要網路。
- **不會聽孩子說話。** 孩子跟著旁白「一起說」時，App 不會聽、也不會錄音。App 不使用麥克風、相機、照片、定位或通訊錄，所以不會要求這些權限。

## 沒有其他公司的程式

KidsAI 沒有廣告，沒有分析或追蹤工具，沒有當機回報工具，也沒有其他公司的程式（第三方套件）。App 裡沒有通往外部網站或其他 App 的連結，也沒有 App 內購買。

## Apple 提供給我們的統計

Apple 會提供我們 App Store 下載次數等彙總統計。如果家長在裝置的「設定＞隱私權與安全性＞分析與改進」打開「與 App 開發者分享」，Apple 也可能提供我們匿名的當機報告和使用次數統計。這些資料不含姓名或裝置識別碼，我們無法知道它們來自哪個孩子或哪台裝置，只用來修正問題。Apple 如何處理這些資訊，依 Apple 的隱私權政策。

## 兒童隱私

KidsAI 是為兒童設計的 App。因為 App 不收集任何資料，所以沒有孩子的資料可以查閱、更正、匯出或刪除。刪除 App 時，App 會從裝置移除；KidsAI 不存遊戲進度或任何孩子的資料，所以 iCloud 或電腦備份裡也沒有這些資料。

## 政策變更

如果之後的版本會用到任何資料，我們會在推出前更新這份政策和生效日期，並在更新說明寫明改了什麼；涉及孩子的資料時，會依法律要求先取得家長同意才開始使用。

# KidsAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: <release date>
Applies to: KidsAI 0.1 and all later versions until this policy is next updated

KidsAI is developed and provided by <developer name> ("we"). It is an app for children aged 6–8 (5-year-olds can play together with an adult) that helps them understand that "AI guesses, can be wrong, and can be taught." We designed the app to collect no data at all.

## What we collect

**The app itself collects nothing.** KidsAI does not collect, store, or share any personal data or usage data. This includes names, voices, photos, location, contact information, and device identifiers of children or parents. We also do not collect what children choose in the app or how long they play.

## How the app works

- **No internet.** All levels, pictures, and text are built into the app. The app never connects to the internet and never sends anything off the device.
- **Progress is not saved.** A child's current level and unlocked islands exist only in the app's memory while it is running; the app never stores them on the device. Progress is cleared when the app is swiped away in the app switcher, when the device restarts, or when the system closes the app in the background, and the child starts over next time. Switching to another app and coming back usually keeps the progress.
- **Narration uses the device's built-in voice.** Narration is spoken by the speech synthesis built into Apple devices, on the device itself (it is not a recorded human voice), without the internet.
- **The app does not listen to children.** When children "say it together" with the narration, the app does not listen or record. The app does not use the microphone, camera, photos, location, or contacts, so it never asks for these permissions.

## No code from other companies

KidsAI has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no crash-reporting tools, and no code from other companies (third-party libraries). The app has no links to outside websites or other apps, and no in-app purchases.

## Statistics Apple provides to us

Apple provides us with aggregate App Store statistics such as download counts. If a parent has turned on "Share With App Developers" (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may also share anonymous crash reports and aggregate usage statistics with us. These contain no names or device identifiers, and we cannot tell which child or device they came from. We use them only to fix problems. How Apple handles this information is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## Children's privacy

KidsAI is made for children. Because the app collects no data, there is no child data to review, correct, export, or delete. Deleting the app removes it from the device. KidsAI does not store game progress or any child's data, so iCloud or computer backups contain none of it either.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version uses any data, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released, and describe the change in the release notes. If a change involves children's data, we will obtain parental consent before using it, as the law requires.
